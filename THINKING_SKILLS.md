# Jason’s Thinking Skills

The uploaded NSW Department of Education OC Thinking Skills Practice Test 1 contains 30 questions. This practice version keeps their original order and splits them into four shorter quests: Q1–8, Q9–16, Q17–23 and Q24–30. Each quest allows 15 minutes for solving and another five minutes for checking. This is a practice pacing estimate, not the official test format or a guarantee that each child will finish in 15 minutes.

Question text comes from the supplied PDF. Original question images retain diagrams and tables, and can be opened larger. The matching official answer key is https://education.nsw.gov.au/content/dam/main-education/schooling/parents-and-carers/choosing-a-school-setting/selective-high-schools-and-opportunity-classes-parents/documents/oc-practice-tests/oc-practice-test-1/3c_OC_-_Thinking_Skills_Answer_Key.pdf . Original PDF pages are recorded in thinking-data.js. Images and questions © NSW Department of Education.

Jason can flag questions, revisit answers during checking, continue unfinished work and review completed quests in My work. Starting a new quest preserves all earlier attempts. Answers are shown only after completion. The model retains the original first selection, the answer before checking, final selections, timestamps, flags and active time per question. The existing private journal also records answer changes and question time. Merely opening the subject page does not start or complete an attempt. Deadlines persist through reload; leaving the page does not pause the countdown.

Local key: jason-thinking-v1. The private Jason progress snapshot gains a thinking field; maths, English, piano and other fields are preserved. Existing authenticated D1 access is reused; no Worker/schema changes are needed. Cloud-locked/offline sessions keep local progress, and automatic sync retries after focus, reconnect and every 30 seconds. The existing parent access controls remain on the roadmap, outside the activity. Sync tests use mocked credentials and synthetic records, not real learner data.

Validation: eight automated model/sync tests cover all 30 questions, 15+5 deadlines, preserved attempts, first and revised selections, cross-device merging, locked access and revision-conflict retries. Chromium at 390px verifies flags, checking, saved history after reload, diagram loading and no horizontal page overflow.
