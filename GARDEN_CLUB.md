# Emily’s Garden Club

Parent-approved adventure: Zoe, Taylor and Gabby’s Garden Club. Six story chapters retain the approved length and wording. Eleven multiple-choice tasks cover message purpose, vowel spellings, ruler length, a missing additive part, area, capacity, mass, equal sharing, pronoun reference, plural spelling and contrasting ideas. Question twelve is an invitation-writing task with a place and something to bring; it has no automatic correctness judgement. Emily can revise her invitation, with the first submitted text retained separately.

The ruler shows the stick from 3 cm to 9 cm. Mats use identical square units. Capacity compares four and six full cups of the same size. Two balanced scales compare six and nine identical mass units. Diagrams have accessible descriptions, and question metadata retains their textual context. Source alignment: https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/english/english-k-10-2022/outcomes and https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/mathematics/mathematics-k-10-2022/outcomes . These are original activities linked to selected Stage 1 outcomes, not official assessment questions or a complete outcome assessment. The parent reported that Emily enjoyed a supported challenge; shared reading and hints remain available, without a timer.

Local storage uses emily-garden-v1. Authenticated private progress stores it in the garden snapshot field. The existing private journal records first answers and subsequent choices, hints, active question time, invitation drafts and completion with activity ID garden-club. First-wrong graded skills feed My Next Challenge through new variants; ungraded writing is excluded. Earlier pony, reading, maths and piano records are preserved. No Worker/schema change is required. Existing cloud access must be unlocked; offline or locked work remains local.

Completed chapters allow both questions to be reviewed. Welcome, question and ending screens are mutually exclusive. The hub uses five progress stars and labels completed challenges rather than asserting mastery.

Validation: 54 targeted tests pass across cloud synchronization, adaptive first-answer practice, pony preservation, Garden Club data/model and DOM/state flows. Checks include both-device merges, writing revisions, first-wrong retention, all twelve questions, completion, review and diagram reset. HTML IDs are unique. Tests use synthetic local or mocked private progress; no real learner records are changed. Full browser visual QA was unavailable in the managed environment, so no new browser screenshot is claimed.
